Working closely with product and engineering, I helped shape the underlying model and designed the end-to-end experience across mobile and web.</p>
  </WorkIntro>

  <WorkText title="Everything in one place">

Absences was organized around three focused views: Summary, Requests and Balances.

Together, they gave employees a clear overview of their time off, a place to follow current and past requests, and direct access to their remaining balances — without jumping between different tools.

</WorkText>

Each step focused on the information needed in that moment, keeping an administrative process lightweight enough to complete on the go.
