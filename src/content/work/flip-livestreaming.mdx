  <WorkText title="Make livestreaming feel like content">

I proposed treating livestreams like regular Flip posts instead of creating a separate product around them. A scheduled post became the livestream when it started, while the recording and live chat remained afterward as the post video and comments.

This created a continuous experience for users while letting engineering build on concepts the platform already had.

</WorkText>
</div>

Behind the minimal interface was a large system of live states and interactions — from reactions, screen sharing and moderation to adaptive layouts, fullscreen viewing and chat behavior.

Small details mattered too: opening comments reshaped the stream dynamically, while scrolling through older messages paused the live chat until the viewer was ready to return.

</WorkText>
